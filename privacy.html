Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026. Plain English, no fine print.

FreshRound Leads ("we", "us") is a small business based in Vancouver, Washington. We sell company-level lead lists of US SaaS and tech companies that have announced a funding round. This policy explains what data this website and our lists contain, and what we do with it.

The short version

  • This website uses no tracking cookies, no analytics, and no advertising pixels.
  • Our lists are about companies, not people. They contain no personal names, personal email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • Everything in our lists comes from public sources, and every row links to its source.
  • Any company can ask to be removed. Just email us.

What this website collects

This is a static website. It has no accounts and sets no cookies. We don't run analytics or tracking scripts. The only form is the sample-request form described below.

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) in server logs for security and operations. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.
  • Fonts. The site loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to download it. Google Fonts does not set cookies.
  • Sample-request form. When you submit the sample-request form, we collect what you enter: your name, work email, company, company website (optional), what you sell and your ideal customer, and the plan you're interested in. Submissions are processed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form service that delivers them to us by email. FormSubmit may show a Google reCAPTCHA check to block spam; that happens on FormSubmit's page, subject to their privacy terms. We use form submissions only to respond to your request. We don't sell them or add you to a mailing list you didn't ask for.
  • Email. When you email us (for example to request a sample), we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to deliver what you asked for. We don't sell it or add you to a mailing list you didn't ask for.

What our lead lists contain

Each row describes a company, not a person:

  • Company name, website, headquarters city and state, and a one-line description
  • Funding round, amount, announcement date, and lead investors
  • A link to the public announcement or filing the information came from
  • The company's own published contact page or a role inbox it lists publicly (such as sales@ or support@)
  • Buying-signal notes drawn from public statements, such as hiring plans, new executives, or expansion

We do not include personal names, personal email addresses, or phone numbers.

Where the data comes from

We build lists only from public sources: funding announcements and press releases, SEC Form D filings, and companies' own websites. We do not scrape LinkedIn or Crunchbase.

Who we share data with

We provide lists to customers who license them for their own internal use under our Data License & Terms, which prohibit resale and redistribution. We don't sell or share information about website visitors or people who email us or use our form, other than FormSubmit delivering form submissions to us as described above.

Remove my company

If you represent a company that appears in our lists and you'd like it removed, email coffeebuds89@gmail.com with the subject "Remove my company". Include the company's name and website. We'll remove it from future lists and confirm by email. No account or explanation needed.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
